Return Policy

Eligibility, return procedures, and refund timelines for aether instruments.

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

1. Return Eligibility

Customers may return unused, unopened instruments within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.

2. Condition Requirements

  • In original packaging with all accessories.
  • Proof of delivery reference.
  • Unused and non-installed hardware.

3. Return Process

Initiate a return via institutional inquiry. The customer ships the instrument back; Aether inspects and approves the return.

4. Refunds

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days of receipt and inspection. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to an error.

5. Defective Instruments

Damaged or defective instruments may qualify for replacement; claims require diagnostic documentation.

6. Contact

Returns desk: support@aether.store
