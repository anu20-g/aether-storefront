Privacy Policy

How the Aether Storefront collects, uses, stores, and protects information.

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 · Last Updated: November 19, 2026

1. Overview

The Aether Storefront ("we", "our") is operated by Aether Goods. This policy describes the collection and handling of personal information when you browse the catalog, manage a cart, or place an order through the storefront.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account and identity data: name, email address, and shipping address provided at checkout.
  • Order data: product selections, quantities, order history, and transaction references.
  • Technical data: standard server logs, device identifiers, and diagnostic information.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to process orders, fulfill shipments, communicate order status, enforce security controls, and improve the storefront experience. We do not sell personal information.

4. Payment Data & Simulated Authorization

The Aether Storefront implements a simulated payment authorization. No real payment card data is transmitted to a live processor, stored on our servers, or used outside the authorization step. Card numbers are validated using the Luhn algorithm and brand-checked; only the card brand and last four digits are retained in order records. Test approval numbers: 4242 4242 4242 4242; test decline numbers: 4000 0000 0000 0002.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to persist cart state between sessions. Server-side order records are retained only as long as required for fulfillment, accounting, and legal obligations.

6. Data Sharing

We share shipping details only with the logistics partner required to deliver your order, and with regulatory authorities when legally mandated.

7. Security & Retention

Data is stored with access controls and encrypted in transit. Retention periods follow applicable accounting and tax obligations.

8. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data held about you by contacting the institutional office.
